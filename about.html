About Us

The Institute of Science and Information Technology is a pioneering private polytechnic institute approved by the Bangladesh Government and Technical Education Board (BTEB). The institute was established in the year of 2000 to develop mid-level skilled engineers by giving more importance to practical education as well as text-book based education in information technology-based globalization.

Institute Of Science and Information Technology is one of the best polytechnic institutes in Bangladesh. The company has been run by a few skilled engineers since its inception.
